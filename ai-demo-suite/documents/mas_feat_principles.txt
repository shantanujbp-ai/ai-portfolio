MONETARY AUTHORITY OF SINGAPORE - FEAT PRINCIPLES
FAIRNESS, ETHICS, ACCOUNTABILITY AND TRANSPARENCY IN AI
Source: MAS FEAT Principles, 2019, applicable to financial institutions in Singapore

PURPOSE
The MAS FEAT Principles provide guidance to financial institutions using AI and
data analytics in Singapore. They establish baseline standards for responsible
deployment of AI in financial services.

PRINCIPLE 1 - FAIRNESS
AI models must treat customers fairly and avoid bias in outcomes.
Credit scoring, insurance underwriting, and investment advisory AI must
not systematically disadvantage any group of customers.
Financial institutions must regularly test AI models for discriminatory outcomes.
Models must be validated across demographic segments before deployment.
Remediation must be applied when unfair outcomes are detected.

PRINCIPLE 2 - ETHICS
AI applications must align with ethical norms and societal values.
Financial institutions must establish an ethics framework for AI governance.
AI must not be used for predatory pricing, discriminatory practices, or
market manipulation.
Human judgment must be retained for decisions with significant customer impact.
Ethics review must be conducted before deploying new AI applications.

PRINCIPLE 3 - ACCOUNTABILITY
Financial institutions must be accountable for AI decisions affecting customers.
A human must be responsible and accountable for every AI model in production.
Model risk management frameworks must cover AI and ML models.
Board oversight of AI strategy and risk is required.
Incident reporting and response procedures must cover AI system failures.
Customers must have access to human review of automated decisions.

PRINCIPLE 4 - TRANSPARENCY
Financial institutions must be transparent about their use of AI.
Customers must be informed when AI is used in decisions affecting them.
Explanations of AI decisions must be available upon customer request.
Model documentation must cover purpose, training data, and known limitations.
Significant changes to AI models must be disclosed where they affect customer outcomes.

AI GOVERNANCE REQUIREMENTS
Designated AI governance function with board reporting.
Model inventory covering all AI models in production.
Regular model validation by independent function.
Explainability capability for customer-facing AI decisions.
Ongoing monitoring of model performance and fairness metrics.

IMPLICATIONS FOR FX AND TREASURY AI
AI-powered FX advisory systems must be explainable to clients.
Algorithmic trading systems must have human oversight and kill switch.
AI credit models for trade finance must be validated for bias.
GenAI systems generating client communications must be grounded and auditable.
Automated hedging recommendations require human approval before execution.
